## The licensing model, briefly

RedScribe is **source-available**, not OSI-approved open source: the source
is public and modifiable, but the license restricts commercial use.

- **Free** for personal and community/noncommercial use, under the PolyForm
  Noncommercial License 1.0.0 (`LICENSE` in the repo), permanently.
- **Commercial use** (including running it at a for-profit consultancy)
  requires a commercial license (`COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md` in the repo).

<Aside type="tip" title="Commercial use is free right now">
As a kickstart for the community, a commercial license key is free for the
first year, no payment required. Email `redscribe.maintainer@proton.me` for
one. Priced tiers begin once RedScribe has been proven at the scale real
enterprise engagements demand, and nothing about a key already issued
during the free period changes retroactively once pricing starts.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Nothing is feature-gated, either way">
Every RedScribe instance has every feature, whether or not anyone paid. A
commercial license changes exactly one thing: a "Licensed to X" line in the
app's own footer, purely as attribution and never a technical restriction.
</Aside>

## Activating a license key

If your organization has a commercial license, you'll be given a license
key, a signed, tamper-evident text string. Activate it from **Licensing**
in the sidebar (`/licensing/`, Superadmin-only, `licensing.manage`): paste
the key into the field and save.

Pasting an invalid, tampered, or expired-in-the-past key doesn't error or
break anything. It's checked instantly, and the footer simply falls back to
the plain, unlicensed display if it doesn't verify.

## What the footer shows

| State | Footer | Also shown |
|---|---|---|
| No key entered | `RedScribe v<version>` | Nothing |
| **Active** (valid, not near expiry) | `RedScribe v<version> — Licensed to <Org>` | Nothing |
| **Expiring soon** (≤30 days left) | Same "Licensed to" line | An amber banner, **Superadmin-only**, across the whole app, saying your commercial license for `<Org>` expires in `N` days, with a Renew link. |
| **Expired** | `RedScribe v<version> — License expired (<Org>)` | A red banner, **Superadmin-only** |

<Aside type="note" title="Expiry never blocks anything">
An expired key is still a genuinely valid, authentically-signed key. It
just stops being displayed as "currently licensed." No feature is disabled,
no workflow is blocked, and no one outside Superadmin ever sees an expiry
banner. This is pure attribution, consistent with the no-feature-gating
policy above. If your license has expired, contact whoever issued it for a
renewed key. That's an offline process outside RedScribe itself.
</Aside>
